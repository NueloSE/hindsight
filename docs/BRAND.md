# Hindsight: brand and design direction

**Direction:** editorial case file. Hindsight reads like an analyst's memo about your trading, not a trading terminal.
Most entries in this space are dark neon dashboards; Hindsight is warm paper and ink.

## Voice
Calm, direct, specific. A good coach, not a hype account.
- Lead with the trader's own numbers: "25 of your 74 closed-market buys…", never "Unlock your potential".
- Say plainly when a habit hasn't cost money, and when a rule trades return for lower risk.
- No emojis, no exclamation marks, no "AI-powered" badges. "Not financial advice" where decisions are made.

## Type
- **Headlines:** Newsreader (serif), weights 500/600, tight tracking.
- **Body and UI:** Geist Sans, 400/500.
- **Numbers:** Geist Mono with tabular figures, so columns line up.
Max 3 weights on a page; 5 sizes.

## Motif
Evidence is highlighted like a marker pen on a printed page: habit-linked trades, cited numbers and
"why" sentences get a soft marker underline. It's how Hindsight shows its receipts.

## Shape and motion
- Radius by role: 2px for inputs/tags, 6px for panels, full for status dots. Never one radius everywhere.
- Shadows: almost none; hairline rules separate sections.
- Motion: 120–200ms, custom ease-out, entry slower than exit, no bounce.
